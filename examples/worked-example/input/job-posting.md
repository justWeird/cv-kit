# Backend Engineer (Python) — Meridian Logistics BV

*A fictional posting, written for the cv-kit worked example. Meridian Logistics BV does not exist.*

**Location:** Amsterdam, hybrid (2 days in office)
**Team:** Platform, 6 engineers

## About us

Meridian Logistics builds route planning software for mid-size freight operators across the
Benelux. Around 40 carriers plan roughly 12,000 deliveries a day on our platform. We started in
2019 and we are 55 people.

## The role

Our routing service was written fast in 2020 and it has held up better than it should have. It
now takes 40 seconds to replan a large carrier's day, and our biggest customers replan four or
five times a morning. We need someone to make that fast, and then to keep it fast as we add
carriers.

You will:

- Own the routing service end to end, from the API down to the query plans
- Profile and fix performance problems in Python and PostgreSQL
- Design and build the async job pipeline that replaces our current synchronous replan
- Work directly with the carriers' operations teams to understand what they actually do

## What we are looking for

- Strong Python, ideally with FastAPI or a similar async framework
- Real PostgreSQL experience: you can read a query plan and you have fixed a slow one
- Experience with background job processing (Celery, RQ, or similar)
- Comfort with Docker and CI/CD pipelines
- You have worked on a system where performance was a product requirement, not an afterthought
- Fluent English. Dutch is welcome but not required.

## Nice to have

- Experience with AWS
- Exposure to logistics, routing, or optimisation problems
- Kubernetes

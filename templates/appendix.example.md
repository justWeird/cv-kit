# Appendix — Company & Project Context

Grounding context for every company and project in your master CV. The `/jd` skill reads this
file before writing any Context line on a tailored CV. It also reads it before grounding a
cover letter in something real about a target employer.

**The rule: if a company or project is not listed here, do not guess what it does.** A skill
that hits a missing name stops and asks you. Once you answer, it adds the row here, so the next
application does not need to ask again.

Keep the Source column filled in. It is what separates a fact you confirmed from a fact
somebody inferred from a company name.

## Companies / employers

Every employer on your master CV. Write what the company actually does, in one or two
sentences, in plain language a recruiter outside the industry would understand.

Mark a well-known name as self-evident instead of writing a description for it. A CV does not
need a Context line explaining what a large public university is.

| Name | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Example Corp | Builds warehouse routing software for mid-size logistics firms in the Benelux. | You, 2026-01-15 |
| University of Somewhere | Well known. Self-evident; skip a Context line for this one. | Master CV |

## Internal projects

Projects that sit inside one employer, where the project name alone does not say what it is.
Group them under the employer they belong to.

| Project | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Example Corp — Route Planner | Internal tool that assigns delivery routes to drivers each morning. | Master CV |

## Side projects

Your own projects. The `/jd` skill folds these descriptions into the project header as a short
parenthetical, not as a separate line.

| Project | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Example App | Cross-platform app for tracking city bus arrivals in real time. | You, 2026-01-15 |

## Target employers

Companies you have applied to. Fill a row when you apply, from the job posting itself, not from
memory or from the company's marketing copy.

A cover letter that references something real about a company pulls it from here. Record the
stack, the team shape, the office setup, and anything the posting says the company does
differently. Cite the posting and the date, so a stale row is visible as stale.

| Name | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Example Target BV | Dutch SaaS company selling scheduling software to dental practices. Team of 30. Stack: Ruby, PostgreSQL, React. Office in Utrecht, hybrid two days. | Example Target job description, 2026-01-20 |
